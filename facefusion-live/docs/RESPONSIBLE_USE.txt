FaceFusion Live — Responsible Use Terms

FaceFusion Live processes images, videos and webcam streams entirely on your
computer. Nothing you load is uploaded and no telemetry is collected.

By installing or using this software you agree that you will:

1. Only use the face of a person who has given you explicit, informed consent,
   and only replace faces of people who have consented.
2. Never create sexual, intimate or nude imagery of any real person. Explicit
   content is detected and refused by the application.
3. Never use the software to deceive, defraud, harass, defame, bully or
   impersonate anyone, or to bypass identity verification / liveness checks.
4. Clearly disclose that media created with this software is AI-generated.
   Exported images and videos always carry a visible "AI-generated" label and
   metadata. Do not remove or obscure them.
5. Comply with all laws that apply to you, including privacy, publicity-rights,
   election and deepfake legislation.
